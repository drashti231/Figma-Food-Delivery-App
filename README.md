## ✨ About The Project

**Foodie** is a modern food delivery mobile app concept designed with a focus on simplicity, clean navigation and a smooth ordering experience.

The design covers the complete user journey from discovering food to customizing a product and adding it to the cart.

## 📱 App Screens

### 🚀 01. Onboarding

A simple welcome screen designed to introduce the app and guide users into the food ordering experience.

### 🍽️ 02. Menu

Users can:

- Search for food
- Browse categories
- View promotions
- Explore popular dishes
- Add products directly to cart

### 🍔 03. Product Details

The product details screen includes:

- Food rating
- Price
- Product description
- Quantity selector
- Add-ons
- Add to Cart action

### 🛒 04. Shopping Cart

Users can review their selected food items and continue browsing popular recommendations.

### ⚙️ 05. Product Customization

Users can select:

- Small
- Medium
- Large
- Quantity
